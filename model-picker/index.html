How the scores work

Quality (0–100)

An editorial estimate of output quality, drawn from public benchmarks and reviews. A 90 vs 85 gap is noticeable; under 3 points is mostly taste.

Cost score (0–100)

100 is the cheapest option in its category at your usage, 0 the most expensive. Uses a log scale, so a model that costs 10× more drops a lot.

Value (0–100)

Quality per dollar, ranked within the category. High value means you get a lot for what you pay, not that it's the best.

"$1 buys"

The same price in everyday units, so you can compare text, images, video and 3D side by side. A chat message assumes about 2 pages read in and half a page written back.

Prices are pay-as-you-go API rates collected in early October 2026 and change often. Flat chat subscriptions can work out cheaper if you chat heavily; compare against your estimated text cost above. Sora 2 is left out because its API was scheduled to close in September 2026.
