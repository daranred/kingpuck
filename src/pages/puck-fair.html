Puck Fair

Killorglin, County Kerry. 10–12 August, every year.

Gathering · Fair · Scattering

10Aug

Gathering Day

The opening. A wild mountain goat is brought into town, the Coronation Parade winds through the streets, and Queen Puck crowns him King in the early evening. The horse fair runs, the funfair opens and live music takes over.

11Aug

Fair Day

The traditional cattle fair, still held under the 1613 charter and run separately from the festival committee, with music, street performers, children's events, food, pubs and dancing.

12Aug

Scattering Day

The party winds down with family events and street entertainment. King Puck is dethroned around 6pm and returned to the mountains, and the fair ends with fireworks at midnight.

The Crowning of the King

The coronation takes place on Gathering Day. The Queen of Puck crowns the goat before the crowd, and the King is raised onto his stand above the square.

The Sound of the Fair

Street musicians, open-air concerts, sessions in the pubs and fireworks over the Laune. For three days the town hardly sleeps.

Getting There

Killorglin sits on the River Laune at the start of the Ring of Kerry, a short drive from Killarney and Tralee, with Kerry Airport nearby. Accommodation books up early for fair week, so plan ahead.
