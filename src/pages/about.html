The living archive of Puck Fair

Discover. Remember. Celebrate.

KingPuck.com exists to be the most compelling record of Puck Fair: a place where someone can discover the tradition for the first time, where a Killorglin family can find an old photograph, where a former Queen can tell her story, and where a visitor can take home a beautifully made piece of the history.

We separate documented history from tradition and legend, and we credit every contributor.

KingPuck.com is an independent project. It is not the official Puck Fair organisation.

Who owns what

Contributors keep ownership of the material they share. By submitting, they grant KingPuck.com permission to display and archive it with credit. Any commercial use, such as a print in the shop, needs a separate agreement with the rights holder.

To license an image from the archive, or if you believe an item has been shared without permission, contact hello@kingpuck.com.
