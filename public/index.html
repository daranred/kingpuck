Killorglin · Co. Kerry · 10–12 August

Long live the King.

For over four hundred years, a wild goat has come down from the mountains and been crowned King of Killorglin. This is a celebration of Puck Fair, its people, its legends, and its very unusual monarch.

The story

A goat, a crown, and four centuries of August

Nobody is quite sure how it started. Some say it goes back to Lughnasa, the ancient harvest festival. Others tell of a goat that raced into town to warn of approaching soldiers. Whatever the truth, the town has never stopped celebrating.
