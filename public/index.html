The goat. The crown. The legend.

Puck Fair · Killorglin, County Kerry · 10–12 August

Discover the story →

For three days every August,
a goat becomes King.

Every August, Killorglin crowns a wild goat King and celebrates for three days. Puck Fair is one of Ireland's oldest fairs: a tradition of history, legend and community in the heart of Kerry.

Gathering · Fair · Scattering

Gathering Day

10 August

The town fills. The goat is paraded through the streets and crowned King by the Queen of Puck.

Fair Day

11 August

The traditional horse and livestock fair, music, trade and the town at full volume.

Scattering Day

12 August

The King comes down from his stand. The fair comes to an end, but the stories live on.
