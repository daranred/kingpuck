The crowning of the King

The coronation takes place on Gathering Day. The Queen of Puck crowns the goat before the crowd, and the King is raised onto his stand above the square.

More on the coronation

KingPuck.com is an independent archive, not the fair's organiser. Times and events are set each year by the Puck Fair organisers.

Official Puck Fair website

The sound of the fair

Street musicians, open-air concerts, sessions in the pubs and fireworks over the Laune. For three days the town hardly sleeps.

What makes Puck, Puck

  • The crowning of a wild goatDocumented
  • A Queen chosen from local schoolchildrenTradition
  • The horse fair on the second dayDocumented
  • Three named days: Gathering, Fair, ScatteringDocumented
  • The goat returns to the mountainsTradition

Getting there

Killorglin sits on the River Laune at the start of the Ring of Kerry, a short drive from Killarney and Tralee, with Kerry Airport nearby. Accommodation books up early for fair week, so plan ahead.
