Tradition

The girl who crowns the King

Each year a Queen of Puck is chosen, traditionally a schoolgirl from the local area. On Gathering Day she leads the ceremony and crowns the goat King before the town.

For many families in and around Killorglin, having a Queen of Puck in the family is a point of pride that lasts for generations.

Every Queen, recorded
