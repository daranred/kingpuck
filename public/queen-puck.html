Queen Puck

The fair's other royal: a local girl chosen each year to crown the King. He gets the throne. She gives him the crown.

TraditionQueens Through the YearsGalleryQueen StoriesCurrent Queen

The Girl Who Crowns the King

Each year a girl is chosen from one of the primary schools in the district around Killorglin to be Queen of Puck. She has an official Lady in Waiting and attendant girls, chosen the same way. Documented

The Queen has her own coronation first: an evening of entertainment about a month before the fair, where she is crowned. Then on Gathering Day she leads the ceremony at the Puck Stand and crowns the goat King before the town. Documented

For many families in and around Killorglin, having a Queen of Puck in the family is a point of pride that lasts for generations.
